Rules

  • 44 × 44 px day cells; arrow keys move, Enter selects, Escape closes, focus returns to the field.
  • Range shown as a teal band, endpoints solid teal: a shape, not just a color.
  • Sold-out days are struck through and announced ("6 February, sold out"), never greyed only.
  • Season badge is informational; prices spanning two seasons are itemised per night on the review step.
  • Hurricane season (Jun–Nov) adds an info line: "Free date change if an advisory is raised."

Mobile

Opens as a full-height bottom sheet with two stacked months, a sticky "Apply · 7 nights" bar in the thumb zone and a "Flexible ± 3 days" toggle above it.
