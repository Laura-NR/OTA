Pinar del Río · 180 km west of Havana · 2 h 30

Viñales

A valley of limestone mogotes where tobacco is still planted by hand and ox. You stay with families in the village, walk the red-earth tracks with a biologist, and learn how a leaf becomes a cigar from the people who grow it.
