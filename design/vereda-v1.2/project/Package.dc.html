A week in Cuba's tobacco valley with the people who farm it. You sleep with two host families and one eco-community, walk among the mogotes with a biologist guide, learn how leaves are cured and coffee is roasted, and spend one day on the white sand of Cayo Levisa. The pace is gentle: early starts for birds, long lunches, free evenings.

Good to know before you book

Viñales has 4G in the village and almost nothing in the valley. Power cuts of 6–10 hours happen most weeks; both casas run a generator for fans, fridge and charging. Lunches and dinners outside the casa cost € 15–25 a day. Bring € 300–500 in cash per person for the week; cards do not work in Cuba.

Power · cuts of 6–10 h most weeks in Viñales

Casa Yamilé runs a generator 4 h a night for fans, fridge and charging; Las Terrazas has solar lights; Casa Lis has an 8 h battery. We lend a 20,000 mAh power bank per room.

Expect it

Fuel · normal this month

Reinier confirms fuel 24 h before each leg. If he can't, a standby driver is assigned within the hour and you're told by WhatsApp.

Normal

Connectivity · 4G in the village, none in the valley

Download the offline kit before you fly. Daily check-in happens in the morning when signal is best.

Patchy

Weather · hurricane season June to November

If Insmet issues a watch for your dates you can move the trip for free or take a full refund. Mid-trip disruption: pro-rata refund plus free rebooking within 12 months.

Policy

If a casa cancels

We propose up to 3 alternatives in the same village and price band within 24 h. You choose; the price never goes up.
