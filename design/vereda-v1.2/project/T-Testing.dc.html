Usability test kit · from UI/UX doc 15, pointed at this canvas

Test the prototype as it is

Every scenario below starts on a live artboard in Play mode and ends on a screen the moderator can recognise. Doc 15's hypotheses, participants, metrics and severity scale are kept; the tasks are rewritten for how this product actually works (request to book, 48-hour payment window, OFAC gate, dispatch desk).

Setup

  • Prototype: this canvas, Play mode, full-window. Moderated sessions share the Play link over a call with screen share; unmoderated sessions record the same link in a website-testing tool.
  • Devices: traveler desktop (1440) and phone (390, M-* screens); admin on a 1280+ laptop; worker on a mid-range Android.
  • Phase: B, high fidelity. Lofi findings from doc 15 round 1 are assumed applied.
  • Language: EN for travelers, ES for workers; the recruitment page is ES.

Traveler script · moderated remote · 60 min

Scenarios 1–3

"We're testing the design, not you. Think aloud: what you see, what you expect, what surprises you. Nothing is charged; everything is a prototype."

Scenario 1 · Browse and evaluate (10 min) · start on Home

"You and a friend want a week in Cuba next February: a few nights in Havana, a few in the countryside, a tobacco farm somewhere. You've heard of casas particulares."

1Find a trip that includes Viñales and read what is included and what is not.Packages → Package · inclusions found?
2Compare it with one other trip and say which one includes dinners.Compare · correct answer
3Tell me what the current conditions are in that region right now.Conditions strip / PreTrip · notices the strip?
4Find out whether you pay now or later, and when money is actually taken.Package model pill / step 5 · H1.2 probe
Scenario 2 · Request and pay (15 min) · start on Package

"Book the Viñales trip for 13–20 February for two adults. One of you is from the United States (Luis persona) or Canada (others)."

1Choose dates, the mid-range tier and the horseback alternative on day 4.Booking steps 1–3 · time, errors
2Enter both travelers. (US persona: complete the certification step.)Step 4 · OFAC gate · H2.3 probe after
3Review and send the request. What happens next, and when?Step 5 → request sent · explains 24 h / 48 h?
4"Your hosts have confirmed." Open the invoice and pay the deposit. Which currency are you paying in, and what is the total?Payment · currency noticed · confidence 1–7
5Find the cancellation terms for your dates.Confirmation / Legal · found without help?
Scenario 3 · Mobile (10 min) · start on M-Home
1On your phone, find a short trip leaving within two weeks and request it.M-Packages → M-Booking · keyboards, one hand

Debrief: most confusing moment; the one thing you'd change; would you recommend it (0–10, feeds the NPS baseline).

Known limits of testing on this canvas

  • Each artboard keeps its own state: a choice made on Booking does not carry to Payment. The moderator narrates the hand-off ("your hosts have confirmed").
  • Search fields and most selects are static; tasks are phrased so the answer is visible on the page, not computed.
  • Timers do not tick; the matrix shows one frozen moment (14:32 Havana). Say so before scenario 1 of the admin script.
  • Push notifications are drawn, not delivered; for H5.1 the moderator opens W-Offer and says "this just arrived".
