What Viñales is, and isn't

The valley is a UNESCO cultural landscape and a working farm district of about 28,000 people. The village has one main street, a church square and a few hundred licensed casas; the valley has farms, caves and birds. Days are slow and early; nights are dark when the grid is down, and the stars are the compensation.

It is not a resort and not a theme park. Horse rides and farm visits are run by families who also farm; prices are set with them, and tipping the farmer directly is normal. Expect mud in the rainy season and dust in the dry one.

Scheduled cuts of 6–10 h most days, usually afternoon and night. All our casas run a generator or battery for fans, fridge and charging.

ETECSA 4G in the village and at Los Jazmines; nothing in the valley. Download the offline kit before you leave Havana.

Roads & tracks

Autopista and the valley road are fine. The track to Cueva de Santo Tomás floods after heavy rain; guides check the morning before.

Open

Weather

Hurricane season until 30 Nov; no watches active. 26–31 °C days, 19 °C nights in winter.

How we plan around it

  • Casas chosen for backup power, 24 h water storage and a host who answers WhatsApp within 30 minutes.
  • A 20,000 mAh power bank lent per room; headlamps in the welcome kit.
  • Activities scheduled in the morning, when both the grid and the signal are most reliable.
  • If a casa drops out, three alternatives in the same price band within 24 h.
