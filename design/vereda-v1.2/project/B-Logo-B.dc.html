Brand · 01 · logo option B · 11 Oct 2026

The fanlight

Over the door of every casa in Havana, Trinidad and Camagüey sits a mediopunto: a half-round window of coloured glass that turns the afternoon into stripes on the floor. It is what a traveler photographs on the first morning, and what the owner is proudest of. The mark is that window reduced to five panes over a lintel: terracotta and ochre at the sides, one teal pane at the top where the sky is. Solid shapes and no lines, so it stamps, embosses, embroiders and survives a bad photocopy.

Built on the same 32-unit grid as option A, so every header, card and icon slot on the canvas takes it without a layout change. The wordmark and descriptor are option A's outlines, unchanged; what changes is the mark. Files in design-canvas/brand/option-b/. Superseded on 11 Oct 2026: C was chosen and is live on every board.

01 · Construction · 32-unit grid

Dashed: the centre (16, 21) on the sill, the four pane boundaries at 36° steps, the outer arc, the sill line and the lintel's bottom edge at y 25, which is also the wordmark baseline.

The geometry, in words

glassFive panes of 36° between radius 5.5 and 14 from the centre (16, 21). The cames between them are 1.3 units of background showing through, offset in parallel so they stay the same width from hub to rim. Never fewer gaps, never a rim line.
hubA half-disc of radius 4.2 on the sill, in ink: the keystone. It is the one element that must stay dark on light surfaces, so the panes read as glass and not as a sunrise.
lintelA bar 28 × 2.7 under the sill with a 1.3 gap; square ends, because it is architecture and not a brushstroke. Its bottom edge (y 25) carries the wordmark baseline.
small cutUnder 20 px the five cames blur into grey, so favicon, 16 px avatars and the QR centre use the three-pane cut: 60° panes, 2.4-unit cames, hub 4.6; terracotta, ochre, teal from left to right. Same silhouette, fewer joints.
wordmark"Vereda", Fraunces 600, optical size 72, outlined: option A's file, unchanged. Cap height 20 next to the 32 box, baseline on the lintel (y 25), 10 units of air between lintel and V.
descriptor"EXPEDITIONS", Inter 600, cap height 6, tracked 0.2 em, teal. Full and stacked lockups only; never in the header.
colourouter panes #A83E24 · second panes #F2B134 · top pane #0B4F6C · hub and lintel #1F1B17. On dark teal: ochre and cream panes, ochre hub, cream lintel. Mono: everything ink.
why theseA solid emblem with four colours in it is too much next to photography; this one keeps three, the two warm ones at the sides and the teal only at the zenith. The ink keystone and lintel carry the structure, so the mark still reads when the colours go.

03 · Colour versions · one per surface

On cream and white: full colour, ink wordmark. The default.

On dark teal (admin sidebar, card back, footer): ochre and cream panes, white wordmark.

Mono: everything ink. Stamps, fax-grade print, engraving, embroidery in one thread.

On photography: never directly on the image; the all-white version on a cream plate at 92 %.

04 · Clear space = the hub's diameter

Nothing enters the dashed zone: 8.4 units (one hub) on every side, scaled with the logo. In the web header that is 7 px at 28 px high.

05 · Not like this

No rotationThe window sits level on its lintel
No single tealTeal is for actions; a teal fan is a sunrise logo
No outlinesThe glass is solid; outlined panes make a pie chart
No stretchingScale both axes together
No shadowFlat; it is already a solid object
No more panesFive, not nine: nine is a sunrise

Also: never the fan closed into a full circle (it is a window, not a wheel); never without the lintel; never the five-pane mark under 20 px; never the wordmark in another typeface; never "Vereda Expeditions" set in running text as if it were the logo.
