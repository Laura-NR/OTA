Havana Jazz Festival, with a seat that is actually yours

Seven nights of concerts across Vedado and Old Havana. Tickets for the main venues are allocated late and sell through channels you cannot reach from abroad. Our concierge requests them for you through licensed sellers, confirms by a date you know in advance, and adds them to your booking. Nothing is charged until the seats are confirmed.
