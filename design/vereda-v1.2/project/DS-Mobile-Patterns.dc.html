Vereda design system · Patterns

Mobile, offline & responsive behaviour

Carlos books on an Android phone on 4G; Klaus opens his itinerary with no signal in the Zapata swamp. These patterns make the same booking work one-handed, offline, and in text-only mode on 1–2 Mbps. No fake status bars are drawn: the real phone renders those.

03 · State coverage

Every screen ships with these states designed

StatePatternCopy formulaExample
LoadingText renders first; skeleton only for media and lists; button keeps width, shows spinner, aria-busyPresent progressive + reassurance"Finding the best trips for you…" · "Confirming availability… up to 3 min"
EmptyIllustration placeholder 72 px, heading, one sentence, primary + secondary actionWhat's missing + how to fill it"No trips yet. When you book, your itinerary, vouchers and offline kit live here."
