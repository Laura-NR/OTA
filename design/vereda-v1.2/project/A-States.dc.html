03 · Offline desk and degraded services

Three strips, one place, never a modal

You're offline. Showing the queue as of 14:20. Timers keep running on the server; acknowledgements and re-dispatches are queued and sent when you reconnect.3 actions queued
Bank statement feed down since 13:20. Automatic matching is paused; proofs can still be reviewed by hand. Provider status: open banking API.
Dispatch engine degraded. Push delivery is delayed; SMS fallback is sending immediately instead of at 60 min. Timers unaffected. Started 14:05 · engineers paged.
  • Service strips stack under the top bar, most severe first, and never cover the dispatch matrix. They carry a start time and what is and is not affected.
  • Offline: reads stay available from cache; writes queue with a count; a queued action shows a clock icon on its row until confirmed.
  • Degraded dispatch offers manual dispatch, never silently waits.
