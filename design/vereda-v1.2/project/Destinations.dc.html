Three ways to cut the island

Cuba is 1,250 km long and the roads are slow. Most good trips pick one region, or two with a flight. Your specialist will tell you when a route asks too much of the days.

West · 7–10 nights

Havana, Las Terrazas, Viñales

Short drives, the densest casa network, music in the capital and farms in the valley. Our most reliable region for power and fuel.

Eco-Tourism in ViñalesHavana Cultural

Centre · 7–12 nights

Zapata, Cienfuegos, Trinidad, Santa Clara

Wetland birding, the prettiest colonial town and mountain coffee. Fuel needs planning on the Topes road; we book drivers who carry reserves.

Havana · Viñales · TrinidadZapata extension

East · 10–14 nights

Camagüey, Santiago, Baracoa

Son, carnival and cocoa farms. Honest note: outages run 12 h+ and Holguín is still recovering from Hurricane Melissa (Oct 2025). We say so on every page.
