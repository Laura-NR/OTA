Why Vereda

Cuba is not a resort. It is a living culture.

Travel with us to engage with Cuban families, artists and communities, not to consume a packaged holiday. We are transparent about the realities of travel in Cuba today: the power cuts, the fuel, the paperwork.

alt: Ana and host Lis on a Vedado balcony

Vereda means "path": the narrow track between farms, the one locals use. A small Spanish-registered agency, an operations desk in Havana, a network of licensed casas, drivers and guides we know by name, and no interest in state hotel chains.

What we stand for

Transparent

Total price before you click. The power schedule before you book. The 3-star review next to the 5-star one.

Reciprocal

85 % or more of what you spend reaches Cuban families. Guides keep their whole day rate. We publish the split per trip.

Specific

Named hosts, named villages. "Bring € 300–500 in cash", not "bring sufficient cash".

How we work

1 · We vet in person

Licence, backup power, 24 h water, a host who answers within 30 minutes. Drivers with a licence and a vehicle we've sat in. Formatur guides.

2 · You request, they confirm

Hosts answer on their phones within 24 hours; nothing is charged until all say yes.

3 · We stay with you

Reality Briefing at 30 days, reconfirmation at 7 days and 48 h, a WhatsApp check-in every morning in Cuba, a line that answers in 15 minutes.

We book

  • Licensed casas and family farm stays
  • Independent boutique hotels (VOYA, Paseo 206)
  • Private drivers, PedroCarr, licensed colectivos
  • Paladares, community projects, Formatur guides

We don't book

  • GAESA or Gaviota hotels; Cubanacán as a rule
  • Viazul coaches
  • Anything on the US restricted list
  • Trips we can't run well this month
