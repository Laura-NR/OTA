Responsive behaviour: what moves at each width

Sheet 01 fixes the grid and the five breakpoints. This sheet is the contract for how each family of screens reflows between them, component by component, so that no separate tablet artboards are needed: every PAGE on this canvas is fluid, and the rules here can be checked in Play mode at any window width. Content-driven, mobile-first, min-width queries only (doc 14 §3, doc 16 §5).

  • Widths are CSS px of the viewport; the admin shell measures the window, not the content area, so the sidebar rule below is applied first.
  • Nothing is hidden by width. Content is re-flowed, collapsed into a sheet or drawer, or scrolled inside its own box. A table or matrix never makes the page scroll sideways.
  • Type does not scale with the viewport. Body stays 16 px on customer pages (prevents iOS zoom on inputs), 14 px on the desk; the large-text preference (Account › Preferences) scales everything 1.25× and the rules here still hold.
  • Test widths (doc 14): 320 · 375 · 414 · 768 · 1024 · 1440. 320 is the floor; below it the page may scroll sideways.

04 · Worker app and Ops Lite (section 05)

  • Designed at 390 × 844; must work from 320 to 430 wide. Portrait only (locked); landscape shows the same layout.
  • On a tablet the apps render at phone width, centred, max 480 px. Ops Lite on a tablet is not the goal: the desk at 768 is (section 03).
  • Large-text (1.3×) and Android font scaling: offer cards and timers reflow to two lines, counters wrap, nothing truncates. The accept and decline buttons stay 48 px high and side by side down to 320.
  • The bottom bar and the sticky action are the only fixed elements; the keyboard pushes content, never covers the primary action.
  • Offline and low-bandwidth rules are on DS sheet 05 and G-Sync; width does not change them.

05 · Emails and documents

  • Emails are a 600 px table layout (section 07). Under 620 px they go fluid: single column, full-width buttons (min 48 px), 16 px body, 24 px display. Two-column blocks (price + CTA) stack. Images max-width 100 %. Dark mode: logo as a transparent PNG, color-scheme meta, no pure-white boxes on dark.
  • Generated PDFs are fixed A4 (section 06); nothing responsive. Documents opened on a phone are the PDF itself, pinch-zoomable; the offline kit's QR links go to live pages.
  • Printing a web page (Legal, Travel info, Confirmation): print stylesheet hides nav, banners and sticky bars, expands accordions, prints link URLs after link text, keeps the licence line in the footer of every page.

07 · The tablet decision

No separate tablet artboards. A tablet is md (portrait, 768) or lg (landscape, 1024) of the fluid pages; the rules on this sheet and on A-States › 07 define what it looks like, and Play mode at those widths shows it. Travelers on tablets are a small share of a casa-based market; the desk on a tablet is covered by the 768 and 1024 columns above.

If a tablet-specific layout is ever wanted, it is two artboards (Packages at 768, A-Dashboard at 1024), not a section.

Decision recorded 10 Oct 2026 on T-Signoff.
