Visa and Mastercard stopped functioning on the island in June 2026. No ATMs, no terminals.

Before you go

Routine vaccines; hep A and typhoid advised. Bring all medicines plus a spare week; pharmacies are short. Repellent with DEET for dengue and Oropouche.

In Cuba

Bottled or filtered water (filter bottle in your kit). Cira García clinic in Havana; provincial hospitals for emergencies. Ambulance 104 · Police 106 · Vereda line answers in 15 min and calls your insurer.

In the casa

  • Hosts register your passport the first night; it's the law.
  • Water and power are rationed: short showers, lights off.
  • Dinner at the casa is the best meal in town; order in the morning.
  • Tip at the end, in cash, directly.

Out and about

  • Ask before photographing people; show them the picture.
  • Politics: don't push, don't lecture, don't post about individuals.
  • Shoulders covered in churches and family dinners.
  • Jineteros: a friendly "no, gracias" works.

US travelers & OFAC

Verified 30 Sep · not legal advice

US persons may only spend on Cuba travel under one of 12 general-licence categories, whatever the channel. Tourism as such is not one of them.

1

Pick your category

Most of our travelers use "Support for the Cuban People": a full-time schedule with private hosts, farmers, artists and guides. People-to-people group travel no longer exists (30 Sep 2026).

2

Certify at booking

Category, name as on passport, typed signature. Kept 5 years; PDF for you.

3

Travel the schedule

Your itinerary is the schedule. Keep receipts 5 years. No GAESA or Gaviota properties.

4

Pay in euros, outside US banking

US-issued cards are declined; pay by wire or invoice. No USD clearing.

Family visits

"Family visits" covers close relatives in Cuba. Our Family Visit package: transfers, a casa near your family, luggage for supplies, Spanish-speaking desk.
