Where you sleep

Casa Yamilé · Calle Salvador Cisneros [no.], Viñales
  • Yellow house with the blue porch, [landmark]; 5 minutes on foot from the church square.
  • Ground-floor room, private bath, air conditioning and fan. Water tank on the roof.
  • Generator runs 06–09 and 18–21 during outages: charge phones and the power bank then.
  • Say "Casa de Yamilé, la de la terraza azul" to any taxi in town.
Viñales centre · casa, church square, viewpoint road

Money, power, phone, water

  • Cash in euros. Cards from Canada rarely work. Budget about € 15–25 per person per day for lunches, drinks and tips. Yamilé changes small amounts at the official rate.
  • Power cuts are normal. Carry the power bank; a headlamp helps. Nothing in your plan depends on electricity.
  • Phone: a Cuban SIM (Cubacel) works for calls and slow data; the Vereda app keeps your itinerary and vouchers offline. Wi-Fi at the square is slow.
  • Water: bottled, at the casa. Tap water is not for drinking.
  • Tipping: about € 5 per guided day, € 2–3 per casa night, rounded up for drivers.
