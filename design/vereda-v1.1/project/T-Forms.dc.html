Form field specifications

What each field accepts, when it is checked, and exactly what the error says in the three customer languages. Rules come from the spec's data dictionaries (§6.2), the booking and payment logic (doc 8), the compliance brief (doc 12) and the flows (doc 7). Field names in mono are the entity attributes the value is stored in. Legend: required optional conditional.

00 · Rules that apply to every form

  • When: format checks on blur, required checks on submit, server checks (duplicate slug, sanctions screening, gateway) on response. Never on each keystroke; never while the field is still empty on first visit.
  • Where: the error sits under its field (DS sheet 02: aria-describedby, aria-invalid="true", role="alert") and, on submit with several errors, a summary above the form lists them as links to the fields. Focus moves to the first error.
  • Never: clear what the person typed, disable the submit button without saying why, use the word "invalid", or red alone (icon + text always).
  • Tone: say what to do, in the second person, one sentence. Example above a value when one helps.
  • Formats stored: names as typed (Unicode letters, spaces, hyphens, apostrophes; trimmed); phones E.164 (+5352413388); dates ISO; money as decimal(10,2) + ISO currency; passports and identity numbers encrypted at rest (spec §8.2).
  • Formats shown: dates "13 Feb 2027" in EN, "13 feb 2027" in ES, "13 févr. 2027" in FR; phone fields show the example for the visitor's market; autocomplete attributes on every identity, contact and payment field (given-name, family-name, bday, email, tel, country, postal-code, cc-*).
  • Lengths: a counter appears at 80 % of the limit; limits are hard only where storage or a document demands it.
  • Analytics: every error fires form_field_error with form, field, error_type (sheet T-Events).
