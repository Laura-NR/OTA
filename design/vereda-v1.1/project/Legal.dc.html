Booking conditions

Version 2.1 · effective 1 Oct 2026 · PDF

In plain language

  • You send a request; nothing is charged until every host has confirmed. Then you have 48 hours to pay a 20 % deposit; the balance is due 30 days before departure (in full if you book within 60 days).
  • Prices are in euros and include all mandatory fees and the carbon offset. Flights, visas, insurance and most lunches and dinners are not included.
  • Travel insurance covering medical care and evacuation is required to enter Cuba and to travel with us.
  • Cuba's infrastructure is unreliable. Power cuts, fuel shortages and schedule changes are not grounds for refund by themselves; our Cuba contingency clause says what we do instead.
  • You can change dates once for free. Cancellation fees grow as departure nears (see the cancellation policy).

  • We collect what a trip needs: names as on passports, dates of birth, passport numbers, contact details, dietary and mobility notes, and payment confirmations (never card numbers).

3. Force majeure and the Cuba contingency

  • Hurricane watch or warning covering your dates (Insmet or your national meteorological service): full refund, or a free date change within 30 days of departure.
  • Disruption mid-trip (storm, grid failure over 72 h, hotel or casa closure): relocation at our cost; unused nights refunded pro rata; free rebooking within 12 months.
  • Flight cancelled with no alternative within 72 h: full refund of the land package or a credit valid 24 months.
  • Official advisory for your region raised to "reconsider travel" or above: free change or full refund.
  • Power outage over 12 h at a stay: not a refund event by itself; relocation if the stay cannot cope, meal vouchers, and a 10–15 % discount on a future trip.

Sanctions notice

US sanctions and how they touch your booking

Reviewed with counsel · 30 Sep 2026 · not legal advice

In plain language

  • Vereda is a Spanish company. We settle payments outside the US banking system and never in US dollars.
  • If you are a US citizen or resident, or travelling on a US passport, US law allows spending on Cuba travel only under one of 12 general-licence categories, whatever the booking channel. You certify your category before paying, and we keep that record for 5 years.
  • We do not book entities on the US restricted list, including GAESA and Gaviota properties.
  • Visa and Mastercard do not work inside Cuba for anyone. Bring cash in euros or Canadian dollars.
  • Regulations change. We check weekly and will tell you if something affects a confirmed booking.
