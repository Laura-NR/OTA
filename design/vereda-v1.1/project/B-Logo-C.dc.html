Brand · 01 · logo option C · 11 Oct 2026

The name, set once

A vereda is a path, and the word is already lovely: six letters, one ascender, a rhythm like footsteps. Option C makes the name the logo. It is set once in the display cut of Fraunces, the typeface the site already speaks in, with its soft serifs and its "wonky" alternates on; the v is terracotta and the full stop after the name is ochre. The name, and that is all. There is no picture to learn; there is a voice.

Wordmark-led: the monogram "v." exists only for the places that cannot hold a word (favicon, app tile, QR centre). Inter and Plex Mono stay as they are on the canvas; only the logotype changes. Files in design-canvas/brand/option-c/. Superseded on 11 Oct 2026: C was chosen and is live on every board.

01 · Construction · the type does the work

Dashed: ascender line (the top of the d = 32 units = the box height), x-height (18.95) and baseline. The letters keep the font's own spacing: no tracking, no manual kerning. Width 133.13 units at 32 high, 4.6 : 1.

The rules, in words

typefaceFraunces variable, weight 700, optical size 144, SOFT 100, WONK 1, outlined to paths. The display optical size gives the high contrast; SOFT rounds the serifs; WONK turns on the leaning e and the soft a that make it recognisable. These three settings are the logo; the file is never retyped.
lettersLowercase "vereda" and a full stop. The v is terracotta, the five letters ink, the full stop ochre. Never a capital V: the capital turns it back into a word in a sentence.
the stopOchre, at the font's own advance after the a. It is part of the mark: "vereda" without it is a word, "vereda." is the brand. It is also the only place ochre appears on cream, so it must stay solid and round.
monogram"v." from the same outlines: x-height 20 in the 32 box, baseline 26. Only where a word cannot go: favicon, app tile, QR centre, the 40 px avatar.
descriptor"EXPEDITIONS", Inter 600, cap height 6, tracked 0.2 em, teal; right-aligned under the a in the full lockup, centred under the name in the stacked one. Never in the header.
colourv #A83E24 · ereda #1F1B17 · stop #F2B134 · descriptor #0B4F6C. On dark teal: ochre v, white letters, ochre stop. Mono: everything ink.
why theseThe two colours sit where the eye starts and where it stops: the v opens the word in the colour of the ground, the ochre stop closes it like light at the end of a street. Everything between is ink, so the name is readable before it is pretty.

02 · Lockups · which one where

Wordmark = horizontal · web and app headers, email header, document headers. 28 px high in the header; nothing beside it.

Full · business cards, letterhead, invoices, contracts: EXPEDITIONS under the name, right-aligned.

Stacked · square spaces: social avatars, card back, stamp. Monogram over the name.

Monogram alone · favicon, app tile, QR centre, the 40 px avatar. Never in a header.

Signature line · plain-text and HTML signatures use the full lockup at 180 px wide.

03 · Colour versions · one per surface

On cream and white: terracotta v, ink letters, ochre stop. The default.

On dark teal (admin sidebar, card back, footer): ochre v and stop, white letters.

Mono: everything ink. Stamps, fax-grade print, engraving, one-thread embroidery.

On photography: never directly on the image; the all-white version on a cream plate at 92 %.

04 · Clear space = the x-height

Nothing enters the dashed zone: one x-height (18.95 units) on every side, scaled with the logo. In the web header that is 17 px at 28 px high: the name needs more air than a mark does.

05 · Not like this

No retypingNot Inter, not another Fraunces cut: the outlines, always
No capitalA capital V makes it a word in a sentence
No dropping the stopWithout the stop it is just the word
No stretchingScale both axes together
No colour swapsThe v is terracotta or ochre, never teal
No rotationIt is a name, it sits on its baseline

Also: never the wordmark with a tagline inside the clear space; never "vereda." typed into running text with the colours (in prose the brand is "Vereda" in the text style); never the monogram beside the name (the v would appear twice); never letter-spaced.
