05 · Rules the assistant runs under

Human sends. Drafts land in the composer or the booking thread; the Send button is the person's, and the audit log records "drafted by assistant, reviewed by Ana R.".
Facts come from the record. Amounts, dates, names and states are inserted from the booking, shown as checkable facts under each draft; the model cannot invent a price or a time.
Minimum data. Booking code, first names, dates, service states, amounts, locale. Never identity documents, billing addresses, contact details or free-text traveler notes.
Logged and limited. Prompts and outputs kept 90 days in the audit log; the aiAssistantDrafts flag (Settings) turns every tool off at once; Operations Admins and Support can draft, Super Admins enable.
Language-aware. Drafts in the traveler's locale, Spanish to workers; the glossary keeps casa particular, apagón, almendrón untranslated.
