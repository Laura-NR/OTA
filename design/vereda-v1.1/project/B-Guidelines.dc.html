Brand · 05 · guidelines in one sheet

Travel Cuba with respect, and say how it really is

Vereda sells casa-based, small-group journeys in Cuba and tells people the truth about power, fuel and roads before they pay. Everything the brand produces follows from those two sentences: warm because it is about families, precise because it is about logistics, honest because that is the product. Tokens, components and accessibility live on page 01; this sheet is for anyone making a poster, a post, a letter or a slide.

01 · Voice · warm, precise, honest

Second person, short sentences, named people, real numbers. We say what will happen and what we will do if it doesn't.

Instead ofWrite
✕ Discover the real Cuba, off the beaten path.✓ Stay with Yamilé in Viñales. Breakfast is at seven; the generator runs until 23:00.
✕ We may experience occasional service interruptions.✓ Power cuts of 6 to 12 hours are common outside Havana. Every stay we book has backup power.
✕ Your request has been received and will be processed.✓ We've got your request, Margaret. Nothing is charged yet. Your hosts are confirming now.
✕ Explore authentic experiences with local guides!✓ Daylin walks the valley at dawn with you. She knows where the tody nests.
✕ Hidden gem · time capsule · gritty · raw · before it changes✓ Banned (DS 01 §07). Describe the place; let the reader judge.

Tone by surface: site and emails warm and plain; documents formal and complete (licence on every page); worker app Spanish, direct, no English loanwords; admin terse and literal; social like a letter from a friend who happens to be an operations desk.

Names: hosts and guides by first name with their consent; travelers by first name; the company is "Vereda" after the first full mention; "Vereda Expeditions S.L." on anything legal.

06 · Before anything goes out

  1. The right lockup for the surface (B-Logo §02), at or above its minimum size, with its clear space.
  2. Cream, ink, teal, terracotta, ochre only; teal for the one action.
  3. Fraunces for the line people remember, Inter for the rest; Georgia and Arial in email and Office.
  4. One honest sentence about conditions wherever a trip is sold.
  5. Licence number and the 24/7 line on anything external (spec §8.1).
  6. People named only with consent; no banned words; numbers real or in [brackets].
  7. Spanish for workers and suppliers, the traveler's language for travelers.
