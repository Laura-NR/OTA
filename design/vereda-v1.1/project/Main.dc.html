Vereda Expeditions · Design system v1.0 · Foundations

Authentic Cuban warmth, modern travel-tech trust.

Tokens, type, color, spacing and imagery rules for the Vereda web and mobile platform. Built on the team's Isla Abierta v1.0 specification: deep teal carries trust and action, terracotta and ochre carry warmth, and a cream ground replaces cold grey so every screen feels like Havana light rather than a bank.

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum Mobile-first · 390 / 768 / 1440 Low-bandwidth by default EN · ES · FR first, DE · IT next

01 · Brand attributes

Five words every screen must pass

Authentic

Real places, named hosts, honest descriptions. No vintage-car or cigar clichés, no "hidden gem" copy.

Trustworthy

Total price first, policies visible, booking status always explicit. Never urgency timers or hidden fees.

Warm

Cream ground, 8 px radii, generous spacing, "you" and "we" in copy, errors that explain what to do next.

Culturally respectful

Consent-based portraits, Cuban Spanish terms with context, no political symbols, no poverty as spectacle.

Modern

Strong 12-column grid, restrained motion, text-first loading, every control reachable by keyboard.

Do

  • One primary (teal) button per view. Terracotta is for emphasis and tags, never a competing CTA.
  • Dark text on ochre. Ochre is the focus ring on dark surfaces, the current stepper state and ratings; on light surfaces the focus ring is teal, because ochre on cream is 1.78:1.
  • Status = icon + label + semantic color. Pending is amber, confirmed is green, never only a dot.

Don't

  • No gradients, no sepia filters, no "vintage" overlays on photography.
  • No red/green-only differentiation; pair with lightness and a glyph.
  • No brand colors for status, no success green on primary CTAs.

07 · Imagery & placeholders

Documentary light, people with agency

Natural light, no sepia. Hosts, musicians, farmers and travelers photographed with consent and named where they agree. Honest practical scenes (a CADECA queue, a casa kitchen) build more trust than a polished pool. In these mockups every photo is a tinted placeholder that carries its intended alt text, so the handoff already contains the accessibility copy.

Show

  • Musicians, dancers, artists, families, farmers, hosts.
  • Viñales mogotes, colonial Trinidad, Zapata wetlands, Varadero shoreline with people in it.
  • Paladar dining rooms, casa interiors, Viazul and colectivo reality.

Never

  • Poverty as spectacle; unauthorized portraits; "time capsule" framing.
  • Political symbols, flags, revolutionary iconography.
  • Banned copy: "the real Cuba", "off the beaten path", "hidden gem", "before it changes", "gritty", "raw".

Low-bandwidth rules

  • Hero ≤ 120 KB AVIF/WebP, cards ≤ 40 KB, blurred 2 KB LQIP first.
  • Below-the-fold images lazy; "Save data" toggle swaps photos for tinted placeholders.
  • Video muted loops only, 5–15 s, never autoplay with sound, captions required.
