Why Vereda

Cuba is not a resort. It is a living culture.

Travel with us to engage with Cuban families, artists and communities, not to consume a packaged holiday. We are transparent about the realities of travel in Cuba today: the power cuts, the fuel, the paperwork. We think that honesty is the beginning of trust, and the beginning of a better trip.

Vereda means "path" in Spanish: the narrow track between farms, the one locals use. We are a small Spanish-registered agency with an operations desk in Havana, a network of licensed casas, drivers and guides we know by name, and no interest in state hotel chains.

What we stand for

Reality travel: transparent, reciprocal, specific

Transparent

The total price before you click. The power schedule before you book. The 3-star review next to the 5-star one. Where every euro goes, on the invoice.

Reciprocal

85 % or more of what you spend reaches Cuban families: casas, paladares, farms, guides and drivers. Guides keep their whole day rate. We publish the split per trip.

Specific

Named hosts, named villages, named traditions. "Bring € 300–500 in cash", not "bring sufficient cash". We say what we mean and we date it.

How we work

Built around people, because Cuban hosts don't have booking APIs

1 · We vet in person

Every casa is visited by our Havana team: licence, backup power (generator, battery or solar), 24 h water storage, a host who answers within 30 minutes, a walk to the plaza of 15 minutes or less. Drivers hold a transport licence and a vehicle we've sat in. Guides are Formatur-certified.

2 · You request, they confirm

Your request goes to each host's phone. They answer within 24 hours, you watch it happen, and nothing is charged until all of them have said yes. If one is full, we propose up to three alternatives at the same price.

3 · We stay with you

A Reality Briefing 30 days out, reconfirmation at 7 days and 48 hours, a WhatsApp check-in every morning in Cuba, and a phone line that answers in 15 minutes, day or night. If a storm or a blackout breaks the plan, we rebook, refund or relocate, and we say which within the hour.

We book

  • Licensed casas particulares and family farm stays
  • Independent boutique hotels (VOYA, Paseo 206) where a hotel is wanted
  • Private drivers, cooperatives like PedroCarr, licensed colectivos
  • Paladares, community projects, Formatur guides

We don't book

  • GAESA or Gaviota hotels and resorts, nor Cubanacán as a rule
  • Viazul coaches: reliability is too low to put your trip on them
  • Anything on the US restricted-entities list
  • Trips we can't run well this month: we'll tell you, and why
