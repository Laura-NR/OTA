A map that shapes routes and tells the truth

"Tap a province to filter the list" is something chips already do, so a map built for that would be decoration. Cuba justifies a map for one reason: geography is the decision. The island is 1,250 km long, most travelers know four place names and no distances, and the product's own advice is "one region, or two with a flight". This sheet gives the map three jobs, a state vocabulary, a panel, an interaction contract per device, the data behind it, and a test that can kill it. The geometry is the Simplemaps Cuba file supplied on 11 Oct 2026 (16 province paths with ids CU01–CU99, viewBox 1000 × 342, free for commercial use with a credit); its contract is on T-Assets › 04.

00 · The three jobs, and nothing else

1

Route shaping

Tap provinces in the order you would sleep in them. The map draws the route, shows the kilometres and hours of each leg, and warns when a leg wants a flight or an overnight stop. "Open in the builder" starts the custom itinerary with step 1 already filled. The map becomes the first step of planning instead of a picture above a list.

2

Honesty layer

Greyed provinces say "we have no vetted hosts or licensed drivers here yet", name the nearest active province, and offer "tell me when" and "ask anyway". Every tap on a greyed province is counted: it is the demand signal that tells Vereda where to recruit next (Join portal), so the empty part of the map earns its keep.

3

Conditions now

Advisory level, hours of power cuts and fuel status per province, dated. This is the promise on the homepage ("we tell you how the power, the fuel and the roads really are") made visible in one glance, and the reason to come back to the map after booking, from the travel info hub.

Deliberately not in the map

Pan and zoom · map tiles or satellite · a pin for every casa · street-level detail · hover-only information · the traveler's GPS position · anything the list below it cannot also do.

The test that can kill it

The map is kept only if people plan with it. Measured from T-Events after one quarter live:

  • Keep if at least one builder session in five starts from map_route_added, or if map_province_unavailable produces recruiting leads the desk acts on.
  • Demote to the coverage strip (already drawn on Destinations) if neither happens. The strip keeps jobs 2 and 3 in a cheaper form.
  • Never judge it by hover counts or time on page: a decorative map scores well on both.

Owner: product, reviewed with the quarterly KPI review (doc 13 §6).
